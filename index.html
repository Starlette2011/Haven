    Why this matters

    Most households aren't ready.

    Disasters are getting more frequent and more costly, yet surveys consistently find that a large share of people have no emergency kit and no written plan. The first 72 hours are when help is hardest to reach.

    Checklists are generic.

    "Store water" doesn't tell a family of five with an infant and a dog how much. Haven turns standard guidance into exact numbers for your household and your local risks.

    Privacy by design.

    No sign-ups, no servers, no tracking. Everything is stored in your browser, and Haven works offline once loaded — exactly when you'll need it.

    Haven follows widely published guidance from FEMA and the Red Cross (for example, about one gallon of water per person per day). It is a planning aid, not a substitute for local emergency instructions. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
